Problem Grounding (10 minutes)
Three questions:

Who specifically has the collaboration problem you are addressing? Name a real type of person, not a generic "user."
What do they currently do instead of your tool?
What would be observably different about their collaboration if your tool worked?
Evaluation Plan Draft (15 minutes)
Four fields:

Success definition — "We will know our tool works if…", something observable and measurable. If your tool uses AI, also state here what the AI decides versus what the human decides — this shapes what "success" actually means. If the AI decides autonomously, success has to include "the AI's decisions are correct." If a human stays in the loop, success has to include "the AI's suggestions are useful enough to be worth the interruption." Those are different things to measure, so name which one applies. If you haven't decided yet whether your tool uses AI, write TBD — to be determined by CP1 for this part; that's an acceptable answer at this stage.
Target users — who you will test with, and how you will access them.
Method — structured observation, pre/post survey, or brief interview: which one, and roughly when.
Minimum evidence threshold — what is the smallest amount of evidence that would be convincing?
