# Evaluation Plan Draft (15 minutes)
Four fields:

## Success definition 
— "We will know our tool works if…", something observable and measurable. If your tool uses AI, also state here what the AI decides versus what the human decides — this shapes what "success" actually means. If the AI decides autonomously, success has to include "the AI's decisions are correct." If a human stays in the loop, success has to include "the AI's suggestions are useful enough to be worth the interruption." Those are different things to measure, so name which one applies. If you haven't decided yet whether your tool uses AI, write TBD — to be determined by CP1 for this part; that's an acceptable answer at this stage.<br>
We will know our tool works if it reduces the preparation time for meetings by at least 10%.<br>

## Target users
— who you will test with, and how you will access them.<br>
Professors who need to manage meeting with different students from different programs.<br>

## Method
— structured observation, pre/post survey, or brief interview: which one, and roughly when.<br>
pre/post survey or brief interview<br>

## Minimum evidence threshold
— what is the smallest amount of evidence that would be convincing?<br>
Evidence on improved efficiency of our users, with quantiable data.<br>

## Human-AI boundary
— what does the AI decide, and what does the human decide?<br>
No plans for AI decisions for now.
